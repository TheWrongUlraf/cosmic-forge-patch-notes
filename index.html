03What stays with you

A new month.
A little legacy.

Anything marked Legacy carries from one month into the next. That includes Legacy Stars, Guardian Legacies, and anything else that gains the Legacy label.

Use Legacy Stars to empower your Guardian Legacies. There is now a clear limit to how many you can hold.

Unfading means a Gift does not fade after use. It does not mean the Gift carries into the next month.

NEW   /   CARRYING LIMIT

You can hold a maximum of 10 Legacy Stars.
